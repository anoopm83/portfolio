Which loan is cheaper?

Offer A8% flat · 4 yrs
Offer B13.5% reducing
Offer B is cheaper

A flat rate hides its true cost. Converted to its reducing-rate equivalent, 8% flat costs more than 13.5% reducing.

Effective APR = IRR of real cash flows, after fees & GST
Fintech · Personal financeFlow 1 shipped

TrueCost

What a loan actually costs. An EMI calculator tells you the instalment. TrueCost tells you which offer is cheaper, a much easier question to get wrong.

Insight
Fees and GST cut what reaches you without cutting what you repay, so the true rate sits above the quoted one. TrueCost solves for it and won't rank by total interest when tenures differ.
Metrics
Two north stars, one per path: Comparison Decision Rate and Loan-Monitoring Retention. Only anonymous counts are logged, with no amounts, rates or lenders.
Quality
A pure finance layer covered by 32 tests. Every claim the UI makes is backed by a worked scenario.

Approach

How I build

  1. 01

    Discover

    User interviews, stakeholder maps and competitor review mining. I name the person, not "the enterprise".

  2. 02

    Define

    A PRD with locked scope, an explicit out-of-scope list and the smallest MVP that can ship and be measured.

  3. 03

    Build

    A real, working app. AI goes only where it earns its place, and it's framed as a draft, never an authority.

  4. 04

    Measure

    One north-star ratio per outcome, instrumented from day one, plus an honest list of what's still mocked.
